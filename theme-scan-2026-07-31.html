HIGH convictionDEVELOPING
Evidence

HIGH convictionDEVELOPING
Evidence

  • Apple beat on revenue/EPS but shares fell 3-6% after-hours on weak guidance citing "supply constraints." (MacRumors, 9to5Mac)
  • DRAM spot prices up ~700% YoY per a July 2026 Bloomberg report; SK Hynix's HBM/DRAM/NAND capacity "essentially sold out" for 2026; Samsung raised 32GB DDR5 module prices from $149 to $239. (Network World)
Primary beneficiaries MU
Secondary beneficiaries LRCX, AMAT, KLAC
Losers / disrupted AAPL, QCOM (near-term), broader PC/smartphone OEMs
Timeline Price hikes effective Sept 1; watch Q4/holiday-quarter guidance for pass-through confirmation.
Two unrelated companies (Apple, Qualcomm) used strikingly similar supply/memory language in the same 48-hour window — a cross-company signal, not a one-off, and still early enough that beneficiaries haven't fully re-rated.

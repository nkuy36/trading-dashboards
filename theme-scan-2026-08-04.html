Earnings Signals

1. The AI Capex Cash Paradox Strength: Strong

Companies
META, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL

Five hyperscalers reported within nine days, all posting double-digit revenue growth and further capex hikes. Reactions split sharply on cash generation: MSFT (+8–14%) and AMZN (+7–9%) rewarded on demand-backed capex (MSFT commercial RPO +84% to $678B; AWS +36.7% YoY). META fell up to 10% after free cash flow collapsed 91% to $784M despite a 28% revenue beat.

Implication
Market now discriminates between capex backed by bookings/RPO growth vs. capex that craters FCF with no clear payoff.
Trade Idea
Pair trade — long MSFT/AMZN vs. short/underweight META until FCF stabilizes.
Sources: TradingKey, Yahoo Finance, CNBC, Fortune

Implication

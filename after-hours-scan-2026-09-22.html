Assumption Audit
Price method
TV MCP batch_run(get_ohlcv): Daily bar (count=2) for today's regular-session close/baseline, 5-min bar (count=3-48) for current AH price, per the documented daily-bar-excludes-AH issue. AH % = (5-min last close − Daily last_bar close) / Daily last_bar close. Day % = (Daily last_bar close − Daily last_bar open) / Daily last_bar open (intraday open-to-close, not the batch tool's own multi-day change_pct field, which spans the prior bar's open and was not used).
Digest bug — recurrence
ALL FOUR digest gapper prices (WOR, IONQ, INNV, VOYG) mismatched live TV MCP verification tonight, consistent with a previously-documented recurring scraper issue. Resolution: for each name, pulled quote_get (regular-session baseline) + a fully-exchange-qualified batch_run 5-min series (AH price), then cross-checked the resulting % move against an independent news search.
